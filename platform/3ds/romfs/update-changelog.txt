## Changelog

2.1-E2 — experimental

- Fix a console freeze at the start of courses with fog, including Choco Mountain.

2.1-E1 — experimental

- Include a checked 6 MiB VRAM snapshot in manual dumps.
- Capture VRAM before diagnostic progress rendering.
- Disable scheduled and manual dump captures during AUTO.

2.0 — based on 2.0-E6; changes since 1.5

- Include Menu/Improved artwork and the Improved kart texture companion.
- Redraw all course/battle maps, HUD labels, menu hints and option icons.
- Rebuild Display/Advanced, Gameplay and Pause menus with touch controls.
- Add Solo VS/Battle, AUTO spectator play and four-course random cups.
- Add optional Modern Logic, reverse, rear view and 2x-5x C-Stick Turbo.
- Add profiles, HUD meters, scrolling maps and threat warnings.
- Preserve all five HUDs and the original HOME banner/icon/sound.
- Fix Old 3DS Low/Normal-distance fog hiding track textures.
- Restore Nintendo title copyright and correct native-grid credit lettering.
- Reuse hot textures, resource lookups, eligible static geometry and visual preparation.
- Bound artwork caches and improve archive residency/streaming recovery.
- Fix GPU resource lifetimes, fog/material state, audio wake/DMA handling and HOME resume.
- Correct CPU/items, Battle navigation/standings, results and pause/Turbo timing.
- Reduce settings/timer overhead and unnecessary EEPROM writes.
- Add Stable/Experimental updates, confirmation and corrected version checks.
- Add portable desktop extractors for Windows, macOS and Linux.
- Expand manual diagnostics and separate export pauses from timing.
- Normal play: automatic laboratory captures are disabled.

Features and corrections adapted from Epic0522's MarioKart64-3DSPort-Complete; 3DS implementation references from Epic0522's Super Mario 64 3DS Port Ultimate. Foundation: Harbour Masters' SpaghettiKart and the N64 decompilation community. Updater adapted from Legend of Doom 3DS.

L+R+SELECT takes an expanded dump. SELECT alone defaults to camera distance and is configurable in Developer, including during AUTO.

Simulation remains at 30 Hz; adaptive presentation depends on workload. Physical Old/New frame rates and long-run stability require console validation.
