Experimental 2.0. Features and fixes adapted from Epic0522; updater adapted from Legend of Doom 3DS. L+R+SELECT takes an expanded dump; SELECT alone defaults to camera distance and is configurable in Developer, including during AUTO. The original 30 Hz simulation is preserved, with adaptive presentation up to 60 Hz. Physical-console stability and sustained performance remain unverified.
